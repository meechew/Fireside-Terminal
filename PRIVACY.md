# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Fireside Terminal ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you use it on any supported device or platform.

## Summary

Fireside Terminal does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. The App is a self-contained fireplace animation and music player that runs entirely on your device.

## Information We Collect

**None.** The App does not collect any personal data, usage data, or device data. Specifically, the App does not:

- Require an account, registration, or sign-in
- Collect names, email addresses, or contact information
- Collect location data
- Collect device identifiers or advertising identifiers
- Use analytics, telemetry, or crash-reporting services
- Display advertising or use advertising SDKs
- Use cookies or similar tracking technologies
- Access your microphone, camera, contacts, or files
- Transmit any data over the network

## Local Settings

Any preferences you adjust within the App (such as color theme, sound mode, or display options) are stored locally on your device only. This information never leaves your device and can be removed at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing its data through your device's settings.

## Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party services, SDKs, or libraries that collect data.

## Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. Because the App collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

If our practices ever change, this document will be updated and the effective date above revised. Continued use of the App after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
